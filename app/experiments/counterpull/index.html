Set down, the body sits at full colour. Pick it up and it goes dark — it only exists while it is being turned.
Somewhere past the top edge is a point the cloud is draining into. Bring it round to face you and hold it there — the orb becomes that colour, and the motor refuses, twice. Win at it twice and it stops playing fair: it slides around the orb to keep its distance, and cannot be cornered.
The other way through is the motor's: it knocks faster and harder the nearer you bring an unmarked spot elsewhere on the orb. Stand on that spot and it keeps going after you stop — hold still, the body starts pulsing on the same beat, and keep holding to turn the orb over from there. That also puts the hole back within reach.
